## Work

> Learning comes from education while knowing comes from revelation.  
> Learning is cognitive while knowing is spiritual.  
> **Myles Munroe**

The primary work involved in the journey is that of leadership.  That is what is needed to guide yourself in the beginning and to guide others in the middle and the end.

You need to learn governance.  Governing yourself, your family and your community.  Eventually, you’ll be on the path to guide  your own nation and work with other nations.

> He who is faithful in what is least is faithful also in much; and he who is unjust in what is least is unjust also in much.  
> **Luke 16:10 NKJV**

You need to educate yourself and others.  You can’t teach what you do not know.  You need wisdom — practical understanding.  You need to be able to relate to and communicate with others towards the righteous society.

Each individual is at a different point in his or her journey.  Each one has a unique purpose to fulfill.  Some of them will serve as examples, others as warnings.  You need to walk towards the light and also be able to help the ones in the dark.
